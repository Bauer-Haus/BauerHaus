Message

Private by Design

Everything that keeps your conversations private happens on your Mac. There’s no company in the middle to trust.

End-to-end encrypted

Messages and files are encrypted on your Mac with keys that change after every message, plus post-quantum protection. Only the person you write to can read them.

No account, no phone number

There is nothing to sign up for. You connect with someone by sharing a one-time invite link or QR code, and compare a security code to be sure it’s them.

Built-in Tor

Maximum privacy sends your messages and files through Tor, so relays and file servers never learn your IP address. If your network blocks Tor, Nexa can switch to a bridge on its own.

Nothing to link together

Every message goes to a fresh address only the two of you can compute, so nobody watching the relays can tell which messages belong to the same chat.

Group chats

Up to 10 people, each message signed by its sender and encrypted like a private chat, with new keys whenever someone joins or leaves.

Backups only you can open

Move Nexa to a new Mac with an encrypted backup and a recovery code. We never see the code, so nobody else can open the file.

App lock with Touch ID

An optional passphrase and Touch ID, and Nexa locks itself when your Mac sleeps or the screen locks.

Nothing to hand over

Messages travel through public relays run by different people in different countries. We never see them, so we have nothing to share.

Talking in a Minute

No sign-up and no phone number. This is the whole setup.

1

Install

Open the downloaded file and drag Nexa into your Applications folder.

2

Choose your privacy

Pick Maximum privacy (built-in Tor, hides your IP address) or Standard (faster). You can change it later in Settings › Network.

3

Send an invite

Click New Chat › Create Invite Link and send it, or let them scan the QR code. They open it with New Chat › Use Invite Link…, and the chat connects as soon as either of you is online.

For the strongest protection, compare the security code (Verify, at the top of the chat) in person or on a call you trust.

Questions

How do I get updates?

Nexa checks bauerhaus.io for new versions and asks before installing one. It asks your permission before checking automatically for the first time, and you can turn automatic checks on or off, or check right away, in Settings › Privacy › Updates or with Nexa › Check for Updates…. While Nexa uses Tor, it only checks when you ask. Every update is signed by us, and Nexa refuses one that isn't.

Is the download safe?

Nexa is signed by BauerHaus LLC and notarized by Apple, so macOS checks it before it opens. Your chats stay on your Mac, and keep working when Nexa is on the Mac App Store.

Is an invite link safe to send by email or text?

Yes. Each link works once and expires. If someone intercepted it and used it first, your contact's chat wouldn't connect, and comparing security codes shows anyone who tried to sit in the middle.

How do I move Nexa to a new Mac?

Settings › Backup › Move to Backup… writes an encrypted backup file and gives you a recovery code. Nexa then erases itself from this Mac. On the new Mac, choose Restore from a Backup… on the welcome screen and enter the code. Your chats continue where they left off.

I lost my recovery code. Can you restore my backup?

No. The code is the only key to the backup, and we never see it. Without it, nobody can open the file, including us. Write it on paper and keep it somewhere safe; don't keep it in a note or screenshot on the same Mac.

Maximum privacy or Standard?

Both encrypt everything you send. Maximum privacy also routes Nexa through Tor, so relays and file servers never learn your IP address, and each contact uses a separate Tor circuit. It is a little slower to connect. Standard connects directly with encrypted DNS and is faster, but relays can see your IP address.

A message says "Sending" for a long time.

Nexa keeps trying until a relay accepts the message, including after a restart. If it lasts more than a few minutes, check your internet connection, or try the other network mode in Settings › Network. Settings › Relays shows which relays are working.

Can I lock Nexa with Touch ID?

Yes. Settings › Security lets you set a passphrase and then unlock with Touch ID. Nexa locks when your Mac sleeps or the screen locks.

How do group chats work?

Click New Chat › New Group…, name it, and pick up to 9 people you already chat with; each of them is asked first. Everyone in the group sees the member list and what's posted, and messages are end-to-end encrypted like any other chat. The person who made the group adds and removes people; anyone can leave. To be sure the messages under someone's name are really theirs, compare their member code (in the group's member list) with them in person.

How long do relays keep my messages?

Nexa asks relays to delete messages after 14 days by default (adjustable in Settings). Relays are run by other people and may keep them longer, but they only ever hold encrypted data they can't read. Disappearing messages, set per chat, also delete messages from both Macs.

Report abuse

Nobody can message you on Nexa unless you share an invite link with them. If someone you connected with is abusive:

  • Block them: open the chat, click the … button at the top right, and choose Block. Nexa stops receiving anything from them.
  • Report them: in the same menu, choose Report…. You choose which of their messages to include, and Nexa writes an email to us that you can review before sending.
  • In a group: click the members button at the top of the group, then the … next to the person, to block or report them. Leave the group from the group's … menu.
  • Or email bauer@bauerhaus.io directly.

We read and answer every report. Because there is no central service, we can't read chats or remove other people from Nexa, but we will help you protect yourself and act on anything that involves a risk to someone's safety.
